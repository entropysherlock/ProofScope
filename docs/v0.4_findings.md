- **Lean:** `lean/ComparatorChallenges/CoulombIonization.lean`, `OAI.CoulombAtom.MainStatement`, especially `JointLimit` and `IteratedLimits`.
- **Paper:** `preprints/Generalized-ionization-energies-for-full-Coulomb-atoms-September-24-2026/paper.pdf`, **Theorem 1.1, (1.3)–(1.5), Section 6.3**.
- **Comparison:** Lean expresses the joint limit on integer sequences `m,Z` with `m→∞`, `Z/m→∞`, and two real-valued iterated limsup/liminf limits. The paper explicitly treats potentially infinite inner limits in its derivation from the joint asymptotic and states that fixed-m convergence is not assumed.
- **Assessment:** The observation that real-valued `limsup`/`liminf` have totalized behavior outside their natural domains is worth auditing, but on its own does **not** prove a gap in Theorem 1.1. No concrete admissible counterexample or Lean kernel-checked implication certificate was produced here. Check precise Mathlib semantics and the paper's use of exceptional finite m with an expert.

### 2. TotientCompanionZero — NEEDS_EXPERT; no discrepancy found

- **Lean:** `lean/ComparatorChallenges/TotientCompanionZero.lean`, `OAI.TotientAsymptotic.companion_zero_case`.
- **Paper:** `preprints/An-asymptotic-formula-for-the-number-of-totients-September-25-2026/An-asymptotic-formula-for-the-number-of-totients-September-25-2026.pdf`, **Theorem 2.2 (`thm:companion`), alternative (ii)**.
- **Comparison:** The Lean hypothesis `¬∃ d, IsTotient d ∧ k*d < ell d` matches the no-seed alternative in the paper. Both conclude the count vanishes for every positive x and the weighted coefficient for `0≤s<1` is zero. The paper explicitly asserts the opposite (positive-seed) alternative holds for k=1 and k=2.
- **Assessment:** Thus there is no identified mismatch. It was **not established** whether the zero alternative is satisfiable for any k>2. That remains a separate number-theory question; an unused implication does not constitute a fidelity error.
