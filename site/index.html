01 / Research capabilities

Beyond “the proof checks.”

ProofScope studies mathematical statement fidelity: the relationship between a formal declaration and an explicitly supplied reference. It does not claim to infer the meaning of arbitrary papers automatically.

01 — CLAIMDELTA

Compare claim strength

Identify supported cases of equivalent, weaker, stronger, or incomparable formal statements, with evidence and conservative abstention.

02 — ASSUMPTION DELTA

Trace hidden assumptions

Investigate what changes when mathematical hypotheses are introduced, removed, or replaced.

03 — PROOF-SCOPE AUDIT

Inspect trust boundaries

Examine dependencies, axioms, theorem dossiers, source provenance, and fresh Lean kernel replay.

04 — SCOPEELIM

Reason over fragments

Use supported Horn, restricted function-symbol, and affine arithmetic analyses to explore statement implications.

05 — CERTIFICATES

Attach checkable evidence

Lean-checked implication evidence and separating interpretations where available. Untrusted search is not proof authority.

06 — FAIL CLOSED

Know when to abstain

Unsupported or unresolved cases remain inconclusive rather than becoming misleading confident conclusions.
